FAQ
Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

How does GrowBizz work?

GrowBizz allows business owners to add their business details and Google review link. You can then share a custom review link or QR code with customers. When customers visit the link, they fill a short form and receive 3 AI-generated reviews. They can copy the best one and are redirected to Google to submit their review.

Is GrowBizz free to use?

Yes, GrowBizz offers a free plan with basic features including 1 business, AI review generation, and basic QR codes. We also offer Pro and Enterprise plans with additional features like unlimited businesses, PDF downloads, and priority support.

Does GrowBizz integrate with Google?

GrowBizz generates a direct link to your Google review page. When a customer copies an AI-generated review, they're redirected to Google's review submission page where they can paste and submit their review.

Why do I need the BizSync browser extension?

The BizSync extension confirms you actually own the businesses you register. Install it, sign in with the same Google account, and it securely syncs the locations from your Google Business Profile. GrowBizz then lets you register only those verified businesses — no manual entry, and no one can claim a business that isn't theirs. Download it from the landing page and sign in with the same Google account you use for GrowBizz.

Are the AI-generated reviews authentic?

Yes, the AI generates reviews based on the customer's inputs about their experience with your business. The reviews sound natural and authentic while being tailored to your business type and the customer's specific experience.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. We take data security seriously and use industry-standard encryption to protect your business information. Your data is never shared with third parties, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Can I use GrowBizz for multiple businesses?

Yes! The Pro and Enterprise plans allow you to manage multiple businesses from a single dashboard. This is perfect for agencies, franchises, or business owners with multiple locations.
