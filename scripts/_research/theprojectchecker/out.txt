HOW IT WORKS
From Idea to Delivery in 5 Phases

A structured workflow ensures every project moves through a clear lifecycle with checkpoints at every stage.

1
Project Setup

Admin creates project, uploads documents, assigns vendor and testing team.

2
Vendor Onboarding

Vendor reviews docs, requests meetings if needed, begins development.

3
Development

Vendor builds software, updates progress, submits demo when ready.

4
Testing & QA

Tester validates demo, reports bugs with severity, vendor fixes issues.

5
Final Approval

Admin reviews QA reports, gives final sign-off. Project complete.

Every tool you need to manage software development across multiple vendor teams, with built-in testing workflows and real-time visibility.

FEATURE 01
Project Management

Every software project tracked with comprehensive fields — from auto-generated IDs to priority levels, deadlines, and real-time progress.

Status tracking across entire lifecycle

Testing & QA Management

A dedicated module for your testing team to manage build receipts, testing workflows, defect logging, and version-wise panel reports.

The Story Behind TheProjectChecker

Born from the need to manage a large-scale internal software initiative, TheProjectChecker is the platform that keeps every project, vendor, and test cycle organised.

OUR MISSION
Building the Single Source of Truth for Software Development

When you're managing 30+ projects simultaneously with multiple vendor teams, chaos is the default. Scattered communications, missed deadlines, untested demos, and zero visibility into who's building what.

TheProjectChecker was created to solve exactly this problem. It's the first tool we built — a centralised platform where Super Admins can assign, track, and manage all software projects, while vendors and testers operate in their own focused workspaces.

Every feature was designed around a real workflow: project setup, vendor onboarding, development tracking, QA testing, and final approval. No bloat, no guesswork — just a clear path from idea to delivery.

Every feature addresses a specific pain point in managing large-scale, multi-vendor software development.

Centralised Control

One platform to manage every software project, vendor team, and testing cycle — eliminating scattered spreadsheets and emails.

Complete Visibility

Real-time dashboards give you instant insight into project status, vendor workloads, bug counts, and delivery timelines.

Built-in Security

Role-based access ensures vendors only see their projects, testers only access testing data, and sensitive info stays protected.

Streamlined Workflows

A structured 5-phase lifecycle from project setup to final approval, with automated notifications at every checkpoint.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your name, Email ID, organisation name, and contact number. We also collect project data, comments, and files you upload to the platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to provide and improve the service, send notifications, process payments, respond to support requests, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with trusted third-party service providers (hosting, email delivery, analytics) only to the extent necessary to operate the platform.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit, password hashing, and access controls. However, no system is 100% secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you logged in, remember preferences, and understand platform usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request a copy of your data or object to certain processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When your organisation is deleted, all related data is permanently removed from our systems.

8. Children's Privacy

TheProjectChecker is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or through the platform.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@theprojectchecker.com.
